05 / Visual Guidelines Rules

UI & Layout

  • Massive whitespace and generous padding is mandatory.
  • No rounded buttons (border-radius: 0) to maintain an editorial, high-fashion aesthetic.
  • Thin, sharp 1px borders using rgba(11,10,8,0.12).
  • Oversized type, asymmetric grids and full-bleed imagery drive the layout.

Color Usage

  • Bone is the primary background. Never use stark white (#FFFFFF).
  • Ink is used for all primary text and heavy contrast blocks (like the footer).
  • Oxblood is the accent. Use strictly for italics, underlines, active states, or key CTA buttons.
  • Brass appears sparingly in imagery, foils and packaging details.
