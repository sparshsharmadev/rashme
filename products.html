Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

How often should I use RASHMÉ? +

For best results, use RASHMÉ 2–3 times per week. Apply generously to scalp and hair, massage well, and leave for at least 2 hours (or overnight) before washing.

How long before I see results? +

Most users notice softer, shinier hair within the first 1–2 applications. Visible reduction in hair fall typically takes 2–3 weeks of regular use. Anti-greying benefits are seen over 6–8 weeks of consistent use.

Is it suitable for all hair types? +

Yes — RASHMÉ is formulated to work on all hair types including straight, wavy, curly, coily, thick, and fine hair.

Does it leave hair greasy? +

RASHMÉ uses light cold-pressed oils that absorb well into the scalp. Applying a moderate amount (not excess) and washing with a gentle shampoo will leave hair clean and non-greasy.

Are all ingredients 100% natural? +

Absolutely. RASHMÉ contains zero parabens, sulphates, mineral oils, silicones, or artificial fragrances. Every ingredient is plant-derived and cold-pressed.
