The RASHMÉ Ritual

Three steps.
One transformation.

A simple ritual, grounded in Ayurvedic wisdom, delivers extraordinary results.

1

Warm & Activate

Pour 8–10 drops of RASHMÉ into your palm. Gently rub your hands together to warm the oil. This activates the botanical compounds and prepares them for maximum absorption into the scalp.

Pro Tip

For an even deeper experience, gently warm the bottle in a bowl of hot water for 2 minutes before use. Warm oil penetrates deeper.

2

Massage & Nourish

Part your hair into sections. Apply the oil directly onto the scalp using your fingertips. Using firm circular motions, massage for 5–10 minutes. Work from the front hairline to the nape, covering the entire scalp. Apply remaining oil through the lengths and ends of your hair.

Pro Tip

Use the pads of your fingertips — not nails. Circular massage increases scalp blood circulation by up to 35%.

3

Rest & Let It Work

For best results, leave RASHMÉ overnight. If time doesn't allow, leave it for at least 2 hours. The botanical compounds need time to penetrate the hair shaft and stimulate follicles. Cover with a warm towel or shower cap to enhance absorption.

Pro Tip

Use an old pillowcase or wrap your hair in a silk scarf overnight. Silk reduces friction and prevents the oil from being absorbed by cotton.

4

Cleanse & Reveal

Wash your hair with a gentle, sulphate-free shampoo. You may need 2 wash cycles to fully remove the oil. Rinse with cold water — cold water seals the cuticle and locks in the shine. Air-dry if possible for maximum results.

Pro Tip

Don't use hot water to rinse — it strips the conditioning benefits. A cool rinse seals the hair cuticle.

Expert Advice

Maximize Your Results

1

Consistency is Everything

Use RASHMÉ 2–3 times per week, every week. Hair transformation is a marathon — commit to the ritual and results will follow.

2

Eat for Your Hair

Supplement your RASHMÉ ritual with a diet rich in biotin, protein, and iron. Hair health begins from within.

3

Sleep on Silk

Silk or satin pillowcases reduce friction that causes breakage. Combined with RASHMÉ, your hair is nourished and protected.

4

Massage, Always

Never just apply and go. The massage is 50% of the ritual — it's what gets the botanicals into the follicles.
